Privacy Policy / プライバシーポリシー

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Purpose and Google Drive access

JQQ 1m Backup is a personal-use application. With the account owner's authorization, it uses the Google Drive API through rclone to upload market-data backup archives and integrity metadata, read existing backups to confirm their integrity, and download stored backups for restoration.

Data and credentials

Backup files are stored in the owner's designated Google Drive folder and may also remain on the owner's privately operated server. Google OAuth credentials and access tokens are stored in restricted configuration files on that server. They are not published on this website or intentionally included in backup archives.

Sharing, use, and retention

Google Drive is used as the storage provider. The tool does not sell data, share it with unrelated third parties, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Backup files remain in Google Drive until the owner deletes them or changes the retention policy. The backup process does not automatically delete stored backups.

Access control and deletion

The owner can revoke the application's Google access from Google Account settings and delete backup files from Google Drive. Revoking access does not by itself delete previously stored files. The owner controls the storage and use of this personal application.
